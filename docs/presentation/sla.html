SLA and escalation

A case is not complete until its deadline is visible.

SLA

Priority policies calculate a resolution deadline at intake, then surface overdue work in role dashboards.

01 / MONITOR

Deadline is calculated

Every active complaint carries a target date derived from its priority and policy.

02 / ESCALATE

Risk becomes a record

Escalation captures the reason, actor, recipient, timestamp, and notification.

03 / IMPROVE

Performance becomes visible

Department and officer analytics show workload, resolution progress, and service health.

Operational value: the institution can intervene before delay becomes a silent failure.
