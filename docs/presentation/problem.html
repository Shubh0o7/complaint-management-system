The institutional gap

Complaints are easy to submit—and hard to govern.

Without a shared workflow, an institution cannot reliably answer three questions: who owns the case, when is it due, and what evidence proves the outcome?

INTAKE

Paper registers and scattered email create inconsistent records and duplicate follow-up.

OWNERSHIP

Cases move between offices without a clear department, officer, or deadline.

TRUST

Students lack a reference number, status trail, and visible resolution feedback loop.
